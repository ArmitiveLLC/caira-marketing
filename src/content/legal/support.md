We're here to help directors, teachers, and parents get the most out of Caira.

## Contact

**Email:** [support@caira.care](mailto:support@caira.care)

We typically respond within one business day. For urgent center-wide outages, include your center name and the email address on your account.

## Sign in

- **Web portal:** [app.caira.care](https://app.caira.care)
- **Mobile app:** Download Caira from the App Store or Google Play (search "Caira" or use the links on your center's welcome email)

If you forgot your password, use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen or ask your center director to resend an invite.

## Common questions

### I'm a parent. How do I get access?

Your childcare center creates parent accounts and links them to your child. If you haven't received an invite, contact your center director — they can resend access from Caira.

### I'm a teacher. The app won't let me log an activity.

Confirm you're signed in with your center email and that you're assigned to the correct classroom. If problems persist, email support with your center name and a screenshot of any error message.

### Our center is interested in the pilot program

Visit [caira.care/#pilot](https://caira.care/#pilot) or email [support@caira.care](mailto:support@caira.care?subject=Caira%20pilot%20inquiry) with your center name, location, number of classrooms, and preferred contact person.

### Photos and voice notes — who can see them?

Access follows your center's role settings. Teachers and directors see classroom logs; parents see updates shared with them. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details.

### How do I delete my account or my child's data?

Contact your center director first. They manage enrollment records. For additional requests, email [support@caira.care](mailto:support@caira.care) with your name, center, and the nature of your request.

## Report a security issue

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability, please email [support@caira.care](mailto:support@caira.care?subject=Security%20report) with steps to reproduce. Do not disclose publicly until we've had a chance to respond.
